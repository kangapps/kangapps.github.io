Bloceraze

Place blocks, clear lines, and build combos.

iOS

Coming soon to iOS

What it does

01

Place colorful blocks

Drag each piece onto the board and make room for the next set.

02

Clear rows and columns

Complete a horizontal row or vertical column to clear it from the board.

03

Build combos

Clear several lines together and keep your score moving upward.

04

A varied set of shapes

Fit short bars, corners, squares, and other block shapes into the board.

05

Helpful hints and undo

Review a possible move, undo a placement, or choose an optional rewarded video for extra help.

06

Progress on your device

Resume your saved game without creating an account or syncing to a cloud account.
