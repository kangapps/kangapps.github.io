Frequently asked questions

How do I clear a pair?

Choose two equal numbers or two numbers that add up to 10. They must connect in the same row or column. Empty cells can be crossed, but a number in the way blocks the connection. Diagonal matches are not allowed.

What if no pair is available?

Undo your last move or restart the puzzle. Restarting gives you a fresh start on the same board.

How do hints work?

Free hints highlight a pair and explain why it can be removed. When you have used the free hints, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for an extra hint when one is available. Watching an ad is optional; undo and restart remain available.

Does Number Match include ads?

Yes. Number Match includes ads. Rewarded video ads are optional and can provide extra hints. An interstitial ad may appear between eligible puzzles after enough active play time.

Where is my progress stored?

Saved games, records, and puzzle history are stored on your device. Progress is not synced to an account or cloud service. Deleting the app removes locally stored progress.

How can I delete my game data?

Open Settings and choose Delete game data. This removes saved games, records, and puzzle history from this device. Your app preferences are kept.
