Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to use Minmi?

You need Minmi Mac with Siri Chat on macOS 26 or later. Install Tailscale on the Mac and phone and connect both to the same tailnet. The Mac also needs to remain available in a logged-in desktop session with Minmi's Accessibility access enabled.

How are my questions and photos processed?

Minmi sends submitted content over HTTPS to your paired Mac. The Mac checks photos locally before passing requests to Siri Chat. Siri may process requests on-device, through Private Cloud Compute, or through ChatGPT depending on Apple's system behavior and settings. Minmi cannot select or inspect Siri's internal processing route. See the Privacy Policy.

How can I delete my data?

Delete conversation history and request traces in Minmi. Mac-side execution logs are stored on the Mac and must be removed there. Unpairing revokes the phone's access token but does not delete history or logs.
