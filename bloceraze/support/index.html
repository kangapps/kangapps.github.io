Frequently asked questions

How do I clear a line?

Place blocks to fill a complete horizontal row or vertical column. Completed lines clear from the board.

How do hints and undo work?

Use a hint to see a suggested available move. Undo reverses your last placement when it is available. Optional rewarded videos can provide additional in-game help.

Does Bloceraze include ads?

Yes. The game includes banner and interstitial ads, as well as optional rewarded videos. Ad availability depends on your region, privacy choices, and the connection.

Where is my progress stored?

Your saved game and records are stored on your device. Bloceraze does not sync game progress to an account or cloud service.

How do I manage advertising privacy choices?

Where Google User Messaging Platform provides privacy choices, open Ad privacy choices in Settings. Read the Privacy Policy for more information.
