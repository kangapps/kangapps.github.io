Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Overview

Bloceraze: Block Puzzle · Erase Lines is a game by Joonkang Noh at JK Apps. You do not need an account to play. Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. The app includes Google advertising services; optional usage analytics are off by default and are available only in builds where analytics are configured.

This pre-release policy describes the current app implementation. It will be reviewed against the final advertising and Firebase account settings before public release.

Information stored on your device

Bloceraze stores game boards, current and completed games, replay history, scores, preferences, and your analytics choice locally on your device. The app does not create a player account or sync game saves to a cloud account.

You can delete saved game data, records, and replay history in Settings. This does not delete information already processed by Google services. Removing the app deletes its locally stored game data.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app can use Google Mobile Ads to request banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents and stores advertising privacy choices where required. If UMP requires it, Settings provides a way to review or change those choices. On iOS, the app may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can keep playing if you decline tracking.

Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the ad request, Google services may process network and device information, advertising or app-instance identifiers, consent state, ad delivery and interaction events, and diagnostics to provide ads, limit their frequency, measure delivery, and prevent fraud. Google’s own privacy disclosures and retention practices apply. Non-personalized ads may still use identifiers for frequency capping and aggregated reporting.

Optional gameplay analytics

Usage analytics is off by default. If analytics are configured in your build and you turn them on in Settings, the app sends gameplay and advertising events to Firebase Analytics. These may include game start, resume or end, active duration, hint and reward events, interstitial opportunities and results, and banner visible time. Event parameters may include a generated game ID, game mode, rule versions, ad placement, and timing or count values. The app does not intentionally send your name, email, or saved board contents in these custom events. You can turn analytics off again in Settings.

When enabled for a build, Firebase Remote Config retrieves the app’s advertising kill-switch value. Firebase may use Firebase Installation IDs to select configuration values. This does not store or synchronize game progress.

Third-party services

  • Google Mobile Ads and UMP — advertising and privacy-choice management
  • Firebase Analytics — optional gameplay analytics when configured and enabled
  • Firebase Remote Config — advertising control settings when configured

Information handled by these services is subject to their own privacy terms and retention practices. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security, and Apple’s App Tracking Transparency information.

Changes and contact
