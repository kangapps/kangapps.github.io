What it does

01

Match to ten

Choose two equal numbers or two numbers that add up to ten.

02

Clear the board

Connect numbers in the same row or column through empty cells. Another number blocks the path.

03

Fresh puzzles

Play regular puzzles and return for a new daily challenge.

04

Choose your pace

Pick a difficulty and solve each board without a countdown.

05

Helpful hints

Use free hints, undo a move, or choose to watch an ad for extra hints.

06

Progress on your device

Keep your saved game and records locally. No account is needed.
