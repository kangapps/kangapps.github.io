Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sort the candies?

Tap a tube to pick up its top candy, then tap another tube to pour. Move candies onto a matching color or into an empty tube. Sort every color into its own tube to complete the level.

Can I undo a move or restart a level?

Yes. Undo a move or restart the current puzzle at any time.

How do I get an extra tube?

Choose the optional rewarded video in the game. The extra tube is granted after the ad reward is earned.

Where is my game progress stored?

Your level progress and game preferences are saved locally on your device. The game does not use an account or a developer-operated game server. Removing the app removes its locally saved data.

How do I manage advertising privacy choices?

Where Google User Messaging Platform provides privacy choices, open the Privacy Choices control in the game's help dialog. For details about advertising data, read the Privacy Policy.

If you contact support through a public GitHub issue, please do not include sensitive personal information.
