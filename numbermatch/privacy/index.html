Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Overview

Number Match: Pair Puzzle is a number puzzle game developed by Joon, an independent developer at JK Apps. The app does not require an account. Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. The app includes advertising, and optional gameplay analytics are available only when the feature is configured and you turn it on.

Information stored on your device

Number Match stores saved games, completion records, puzzle history, daily puzzle assignments, and app preferences locally on your device. We do not operate a game account, cloud sync, or our own game server.

You can delete saved games, records, and puzzle history from Settings by choosing Delete game data. Your app preferences are kept. Deleting the app removes its locally stored game data.

Advertising

Number Match uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded video and interstitial ads. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the ad request, Google may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad impressions, and ad interactions to provide, measure, and protect its advertising services.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required. On iOS, the app may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before tracking for advertising. You can change tracking and advertising choices in your device settings.

Rewarded ads are optional and may provide extra hints. Number Match also includes interstitial ads between eligible puzzles.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices.

Optional gameplay analytics

Gameplay analytics are off by default. If analytics are available in your build, you can turn them on in Settings. Only after you opt in, the app may send gameplay and puzzle-generation events to Google Analytics for Firebase. These events can include game and puzzle identifiers, game mode, difficulty, progress actions such as starting or completing a puzzle, hint and undo use, puzzle-generation status, and ad measurement such as format, value, and currency. Firebase Analytics may also collect app-instance identifiers and general app or device information. The app does not include your name or email address in its custom gameplay events.

You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings. The app stops sending future events and asks Firebase Analytics to reset its local analytics data. Data already received by Google is handled under Google's retention and deletion policies. See Firebase privacy and security.

Children's privacy

Number Match is not designed specifically for children. Ad requests are subject to the app's eligibility and consent controls and Google's policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us so we can review your concern.

Third-party services

The app may use the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob and UMP — advertising and consent management
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — optional gameplay analytics when enabled and configured

These services are provided by Google and may process information on Google's systems under their own terms and privacy policies. Number Match does not process payments or offer in-app purchases.

Data retention

Game data remains on your device until you delete it in Settings or remove the app. Advertising and optional analytics data handled by Google is subject to Google's retention policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The revision date at the top of this page will show when the latest version was published.
