Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Overview

Boxing Timer is a boxing and fitness round timer app developed by Joon, an independent developer. This policy explains how the app handles your data. Workout records stay on your device or in Apple Health. In versions with advertising enabled, the free version may show Google AdMob ads, which involve the separate data processing described below.

Data Collection and Storage

Boxing Timer stores workout history — including rounds completed, workout duration, and date — locally on your device. The app does not send this workout history to the developer or advertising partners. Optional Apple Health integration is described below.

Your most recent workout summary is shared between the app and its widget through a local App Group container on your device. This data remains on-device.

Boxing Timer has no user accounts or developer-operated servers that store your workout records. The app does not provide your name, email address, workout history, HealthKit records, or workout settings to Google for ad targeting. When advertising is enabled, Google's SDK may process device and advertising data as described under Third-Party Services.

Apple HealthKit Integration

Boxing Timer can optionally write boxing workout data to Apple Health (HealthKit). This feature requires your explicit permission through the standard iOS Health access prompt. You can grant or revoke this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app under Health > Data Access & Devices.

Data written to HealthKit is governed by Apple's privacy policies and your device settings. Boxing Timer does not read data from HealthKit — it only writes workout records when you complete a session and have granted permission to do so.

In-App Subscriptions

Boxing Timer offers in-app subscriptions managed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. All payment processing, subscription management, and billing are handled by Apple. Boxing Timer does not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details.

For information about how Apple handles purchase data, refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

In versions with advertising enabled, the free version may display a small bottom banner from Google AdMob. Ads are hidden while a workout is running or paused, during onboarding, and on purchase screens. Active Pro subscriptions and lifetime Pro purchases remove ads. The app checks purchase status before requesting ads.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ads viewed and interacted with, app interactions, performance information, and diagnostic data. Google may use this information to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate its services. Non-personalized ads can still involve this processing.

Boxing Timer requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party ID, and does not request permission to access the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA). Google User Messaging Platform handles applicable regional privacy choices before ad requests are allowed. Where required, you can review your choices in the app under Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also use your iOS privacy settings.

Workout configuration, workout history, calories, and HealthKit data are not supplied to Google as ad targeting information. Apple HealthKit and StoreKit operate under Apple's own policies and your device settings.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Children's Privacy

Boxing Timer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. When advertising is enabled, Google may process device and advertising data as described above. The app does not provide health or workout data to Google to target advertising to children or other users.

Data Retention and Deletion

Workout history is stored locally on your device. You can delete it in the app under Settings > Delete All My Data or by deleting the app. HealthKit records can be managed separately in the iOS Health app. For questions about advertising data and privacy choices, see Google's policy above or contact us below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
