What it does

01

Five worlds

Explore Garden, Ocean, Cosmos, Jungle, and Volcano, each with distinct visuals and increasing difficulty.

02

250 levels

Play 50 hand-crafted levels in each world.

03

Star ratings

Earn up to three stars per level based on how efficiently you solve each puzzle.

05

Background music

Listen to background tracks while solving puzzles at your own pace.

06

Local progress

Your progress is stored on your device.
