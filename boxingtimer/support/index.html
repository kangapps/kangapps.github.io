Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

In the app, open Settings > Manage Subscription > Restore Purchases. You can also use Restore Purchases on the Upgrade to Pro screen. Sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. If your Pro access does not return, contact us and include the purchase type and any error shown.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Monthly and yearly subscriptions are managed by Apple. In Boxing Timer, open Settings > Manage Subscription and choose Manage Subscription when shown, or open iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase and has no subscription to cancel.

Where can I upgrade to Pro?

Open Settings and tap Upgrade to Pro in the first section. Monthly, yearly, and lifetime options are shown when available from the App Store.

How do background round alerts work?

Allow notifications when you start a workout. Round changes and warnings can then arrive as local notifications while the app is in the background. Delivery and sound follow your iOS notification, silent-mode, and Focus settings. Background alerts use the system notification sound when Sound Effects is enabled. If permission or scheduling fails, the app reports it; you can still use the timer while the app is open. Workouts requiring more than iOS's 64 pending notifications need fewer rounds to use background alerts.

Why do I see an ad?

When advertising is enabled, the free version may show a small banner at the bottom of the app while the timer is idle. Ads are hidden during running and paused workouts, onboarding, and purchase screens. An active monthly or yearly Pro subscription, or lifetime Pro, removes ads.

How can I change my ad privacy choices?

Where a regional privacy message requires it, open Settings > Ad privacy choices in the app. This option appears only when it is required for your region and consent status. See the Privacy Policy for information about Google's ad data processing.

Where is my workout data stored?

Workout history is stored locally on your device; we do not run a server for workout records. To remove your history and reset app settings, use Settings > Delete All My Data. Deleting the app also removes its local history. Workouts already saved to Apple Health must be managed separately in the Health app. See the Privacy Policy for details about ad-related data.

Why aren't my workouts showing in Apple Health?

Make sure you've granted Boxing Timer permission to write to Apple Health. Go to iPhone Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Boxing Timer, and enable all categories.

Can I use Boxing Timer on Apple Watch?

Apple Watch support is planned for a future update. Currently, you can use Live Activity to view your timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

The timer doesn't make sound. What should I do?

Boxing Timer works with your phone on silent, but make sure the volume is turned up. In the app, check Settings to ensure your volume level is set above zero. Also verify that Do Not Disturb or Focus mode is not active.

How do I add the widget to my Home Screen?

Long-press on your Home Screen, tap the "+" button in the top-left corner, search for "Boxing Timer", and choose the widget size you prefer.
