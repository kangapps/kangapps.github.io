Minmi

Use Siri Chat on your Mac from an iPhone or Android phone.

iOS Android Mac companion

Continue conversations

Keep a separate Siri Chat conversation for each paired phone and follow up from Minmi.

Read photos and receipts

Review an image in Minmi, then send it to your paired Mac for local OCR checks and Siri Chat analysis.

Private network connection

Minmi connects to your Mac over HTTPS through your Tailscale network. There is no Minmi cloud answer service.

Voice conversation

Ask follow-up questions while Minmi stays open. On iPhone, speech recognition requires on-device support for the selected language.

Minmi Mac companion

Required to connect Minmi on your phone to Siri Chat on your Mac.

Apple silicon · macOS 26 or later · Tailscale required

Mac releases

Signed and notarized Apple silicon builds will be published here.

Requirements: Minmi Mac with Siri Chat on macOS 26 or later, plus Tailscale on the Mac and phone.
