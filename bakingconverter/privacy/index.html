Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 10, 2026

Overview

Baking Converter is a baking measurement conversion app developed by Joon, an independent developer. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data. The short version: your data stays on your device, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Data Collection and Storage

Baking Converter stores conversion history and favorite conversions locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Baking Converter does not collect, store, or process any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or other personal identifiers
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

There are no user accounts, no cloud sync, and no server-side storage of any kind.

In-App Purchases

Baking Converter offers a one-time in-app purchase managed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. All payment processing is handled by Apple. Baking Converter does not collect, store, or have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details.

For information about how Apple handles purchase data, refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

Baking Converter does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking services. The only third-party involvement is Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases, which operates under Apple's own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Baking Converter does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect, transmit, or store personal data, there is no data to be concerned about regardless of the user's age.

Data Retention and Deletion

All conversion history and settings are stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
